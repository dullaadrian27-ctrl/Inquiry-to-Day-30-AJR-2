Real examples from Pratt Street and Bustleton

Experience is for everyone

Paying parent or not, every person who calls, visits or walks through your door is a future family or a future referral.

The mom who couldn't enroll

A woman getting the runaround on foster care funding. We had no spot and she had no funding. Aliya called her back and spent about 30 minutes sharing everything she knew. "I'll tell anyone to enroll." Aliya asked for a Google review. It came in 10 minutes later.

It can convert today, in 6 months, or through someone she tells.

The therapist

Therapists and professionals see how you treat children and how you treat them. One left a 5-star review about working with the team. They refer families to us all the time.

Professionals are in hundreds of homes. Who do they recommend?

The mail carrier

She dropped off our mail for a year. Every day she saw how we greeted people and how the children sounded. Then she enrolled her son.

Her experience just dropping off mail mattered.

The village review

We say we're a village. A parent wrote it back to us: "They say it takes a village... & that's exactly what they are," naming the buzz-in door and three teachers by name.

When the gap closes, families repeat your promise in public.
